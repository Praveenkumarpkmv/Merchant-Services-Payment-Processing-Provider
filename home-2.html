How processing works

From tap to settlement, engineered.

Every payment that touches our platform runs through the same hardened pipeline: tokenization, authorization, clearing, and settlement — with observability at every hop.

01

Tokenize at the edge

Card data is encrypted and tokenized before it leaves the customer's device or terminal. Raw PANs never enter your application code or our logs.

PCI tokenization Device-native SDKs Vaulting
02

Route to the best acquirer

Our routing engine scores every transaction against live authorization rates, cost, geography, and card type — then sends it down the path most likely to succeed.

Smart routing 32+ acquirers Cost optimization
03

Authorize in real time

Sub-140ms median round-trips with automatic retries, fallback acquirers, and network-level 3-D Secure when the risk profile calls for it.

Real-time auth 3-D Secure 2 Retry logic
04

Clear, reconcile, settle

Captured funds flow through clearing, get reconciled against your ledger, and land in your account — with same-day options for eligible merchants.

Same-day settle Ledger reconciliation Multi-currency
Architecture

Built like infrastructure. Because it is.

MerchantPay runs on a stateless, multi-region processing core with isolated cardholder data environments, deterministic replay, and full audit trails for every state change.

  • Multi-region active-active processing with automatic failover.
  • Isolated cardholder data environment, scoped to PCI DSS Level 1.
  • Idempotent APIs with deterministic replay for every payment event.
  • End-to-end tracing from SDK call to settlement confirmation.
