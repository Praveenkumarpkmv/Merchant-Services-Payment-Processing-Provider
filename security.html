Architecture

Zero-trust, every layer.

No implicit trust between services. Every request — internal or external — is authenticated, authorized, and encrypted before it touches a single record. Compromise of one layer doesn't mean compromise of the whole.

  • Mutual TLS between every service
    Service-to-service traffic uses short-lived mTLS certs, rotated every 24 hours.
  • Least-privilege access by default
    No service can talk to another without an explicit, scoped, auditable grant.
  • Continuous verification
    Tokens expire in minutes. Sessions re-authenticate on every privilege escalation.

Data protection

Your data is never ours to lose.

We design our systems so that a breach of one component can't expose customer data. Here's how each layer is protected.

01
Tokenization No raw card numbers

Card numbers are tokenized at the edge before leaving the customer's browser. Our systems only ever see tokens, which are useless if exfiltrated.

02
Field-level encryption AES-256-GCM per record

PII fields are individually encrypted with per-record keys managed in an HSM. Even a full DB dump yields only ciphertext.

03
Key isolation Separate key per tenant

Every merchant has isolated encryption keys. Compromise of one merchant's keys cannot decrypt another merchant's data.

04
Data residency Regional by choice

Choose US, EU, or APAC data residency. Your data never leaves your chosen region, and is never replicated across borders.

05
Retention limits Minimal by default

We retain only what's required by law and card scheme rules. Everything else is purged on a defined schedule — 12 months by default.

Access control

Nobody gets more access than they need.

Every action inside MerchantPay is scoped, role-gated, and logged. There is no "super admin" without MFA, and no internal engineer can view card data without a time-limited, audited break-glass approval.

  • SSO + SCIM provisioning
    SAML and OIDC SSO for all team members, with automatic SCIM user lifecycle sync.
  • Hardware-key MFA
    Support for FIDO2 hardware keys on every account. SMS OTP is not permitted.
  • Just-in-time elevation
    Elevated privileges are granted for a defined window and revoked automatically.

Audit & reporting

Full visibility. Nothing hidden.

Every authentication, authorization decision, and data mutation is written to an immutable audit log. You can search, filter, and export events through the dashboard or the API.

  • SIEM integration via Splunk, Datadog, and generic syslog
  • Real-time alerting on privilege escalation and policy violations
