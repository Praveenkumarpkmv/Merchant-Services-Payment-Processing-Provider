What we believe

Principles we refuse to compromise.

Payments is a trust business. Every architectural decision, every support interaction, and every product choice flows from the same handful of non-negotiable beliefs.

Trust is the product

Every payment carries a promise. We treat uptime, security, and reconciliation accuracy as the core deliverables — not as line items on a roadmap.

Boring by design

We want our infrastructure to be the least interesting thing about your stack. Predictable behavior, transparent APIs, and zero surprises.

Pricing without traps

No hidden fees, no mid-contract escalators, no "contact us for volume discounts" games. What you see on the pricing page is what you pay.

Humans in the loop

Automation handles the volume; people handle the exceptions. Our support engineers are payments engineers, not script readers.

Metrics over noise

We publish the numbers that matter — auth rates, latency, uptime — and we hold ourselves to them in public.

Ship, learn, repeat

Payments infrastructure moves fast, but carefully. We ship small, we measure, and we roll back without ego.

Our journey

From a single API to global rails.

Eight years, four product pivots, and one constant: an obsession with making payments infrastructure something developers actually enjoy building on.
