Where your money goes

We show you the full breakdown.

Most processors quote a single rate and hide the split. We publish it: interchange goes to the issuing bank, scheme fees go to the card networks, and only a small slice comes to us.

  • Interchange is fixed
    Set by Visa and Mastercard — identical for every processor. Nobody negotiates it.
  • Scheme fees are fixed
    Also set by the networks. The only variable is how much markup each processor adds.
  • Our markup is transparent
    We show you our exact fee on every transaction, line by line, in the dashboard.
